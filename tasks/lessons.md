# Lessons (v2 redesign)

## 2026-07-11 — A vibe reference is not a product category

Mukund said "Erewhon meets defense technology." I built literal grocery
cosplay — "CONSULTING CLEANSE," shelf tags, $0.00 price stickers, "THIS
SEMESTER'S MENU." Hard rejection: "Consulting cleanse? Are you serious? No."

**The rule:** when he names a brand as a reference, extract its *qualities*
(Erewhon = premium, culty, clean, LA-status, coveted) and apply those
qualities to OUR subject. Never transplant the reference's literal product
category, vocabulary, or props. Same failure class as the June "category-
typical filler" lesson — the reference is a temperature, not a costume.

**Carried over from June (still binding):** no numbered 01–04 program rows,
no decorative mono labels, every element carries a real fact, one weird
thing per page with discipline everywhere else.
